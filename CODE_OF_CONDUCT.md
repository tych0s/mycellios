# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Mycellios a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples include showing empathy and respect, accepting constructive feedback,
focusing on what is best for the community, and taking responsibility for
mistakes. Harassment, sexualized attention, insults, threats, doxxing, sustained
disruption and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues
and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be private,
proportionate and documented by the maintainers.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owners through GitHub. If a
conduct report also involves a security vulnerability or exposed personal data,
use the private advisory process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will
respect the reporter's privacy as far as reasonably possible.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1. Its enforcement ladder is
guided by the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
